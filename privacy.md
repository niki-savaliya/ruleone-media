# Privacy Policy – Rule One Poster

Last updated: 6 Nov 2026

Rule One Poster is a private tool used only by the owner of The Rule One Survival.

- What we access: only the account owner's TikTok display name, to confirm the right account is connected, and permission to upload our own videos.
- What we store: a login token for our own account, kept encrypted as a GitHub secret. No other data is stored.
- We do not collect, sell or share any data about other TikTok users or viewers.
- The owner can remove the app's access at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy -> Security -> Manage app permissions.
